# Homework: The Future of Software Education

How should computer science and software engineering education evolve to prepare students for the era of AI-assisted development? Write a short essay stating your opinion on the concrete changes that should be incorporated into the curriculum.

## Submission Guidelines

- **Format**: Use a clear structure (bullet points or short paragraphs).
- **Length**: 3 to 500 words maximum.
- **Authenticity**: Rely strictly on your own reasoning. Do not use search engines or generative AI. If your genuine stance is "I don't know" or "I do not have an opinion," you may submit exactly that.
